One product photo → a marketplace-ready image and a full Arabic & English listing

PhotoGlobal is an iPhone app for online sellers in the UAE, Saudi Arabia and the GCC. It removes the background, sizes your image for Noon, Amazon.ae, Instagram and WhatsApp, and writes your title, bullets and description in Arabic and English.

Coming soon to the App Store.

Features

  • Background removal that never alters logos, labels or printed text
  • Arabic + English listing generator
  • Export presets and requirement checklist for Noon, Amazon.ae, Instagram, WhatsApp
  • Batch mode: up to 20 products at once
  • Full right-to-left Arabic interface, dark mode
  • Human support on WhatsApp

Pricing (AED)

  • Free: 3 exports per day, no watermark
  • Pro: AED 19/month or AED 99/year (3-day trial, reminder before it ends, one-tap cancel)
  • Export Pack: AED 9 for 30 exports, one-time
