PhotoGlobal: GCC Seller Studio (v1.1)

GCC Marketplace Photo Compliance Inspector & Bilingual Vision OCR Studio

PhotoGlobal: GCC Seller Studio is an iPhone app purpose-built for e-commerce sellers in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, and the GCC. It removes backgrounds on-device, verifies real pixel compliance (RGB(255,255,255) pure white borders and ≥85% subject frame fill), and scans product labels with Apple Vision OCR to generate bilingual Arabic & English listings for Noon, Amazon.ae, Salla (سلة), Zid (زد), Haraj (حراج), Dubizzle, Shopify, Instagram, TikTok Shop, and WhatsApp.

Key Capabilities

  • Real Pixel & Alpha-Bounding-Box Compliance Inspector: Measures border RGB values and non-transparent cutout bounding boxes with an interactive 70%–95% subject scale slider and natural ground shadow toggle.
  • 10 GCC & Global Marketplace Presets: Noon (2000×2000), Amazon.ae (2000×2000), Salla سلة (1500×1500), Zid زد (1600×1600), Haraj حراج (1600×1200), Dubizzle (1600×1200), Shopify (2048×2048), Instagram Shop (1080×1350), TikTok Shop (1080×1920), and WhatsApp Catalog (1024×1024).
  • On-Device Vision OCR Seller Copy Studio: Scan English + Arabic packaging labels with VNRecognizeTextRequest to auto-fill product attributes and generate ready-to-paste bilingual titles, bullet points, and SEO keywords.
  • Batch Mode: Process up to 20 products at once on your iPhone.
  • 100% On-Device Privacy: Product photos are processed locally on your iPhone and never uploaded to external servers.
