Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

PhotoGlobal ("we", "us") makes an iPhone app that turns product photos into marketplace-ready images and bilingual listings. This policy explains what data the app handles. Short version: your photos stay on your phone, we don't collect any data, and we don't track you.

1. Photos

Background removal and image editing run entirely on your device using Apple's on-device Vision framework. Your photos never leave your phone. The app only reads photos you choose or take, and only saves images to your library when you export them.

2. Listing text

Listings are written on your device from built-in templates. The product details you type never leave your phone — the app has no server and sends nothing to us or to any AI provider.

3. Purchases

Subscriptions and the Starter Pack are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We never see your payment card or Apple ID. Apple tells the app only whether you have an active purchase.

4. Data stored on your device

Your app settings, daily free-export count, Starter Pack credits and thumbnails of your recent exports are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.

5. Notifications

If you start a free trial, the app can schedule a local reminder 24 hours before the trial ends. It is created on your phone; no push service or server is involved.

6. No tracking, no ads

PhotoGlobal contains no advertising, no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and does not use Apple's advertising identifier. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

7. Sharing

We don't collect personal data, so there is nothing for us to sell, rent or share.

8. Children

The app is meant for sellers and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect children's data.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including the UAE PDPL, Saudi PDPL and GDPR) you have rights over your personal data. PhotoGlobal has no accounts and no server, so we hold no data about you; everything the app stores is on your iPhone and is removed when you delete the app. Questions? Contact us below.

10. Contact

WhatsApp: +1 914 650 2041 · Web: PhotoGlobal website

11. Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above, and for significant changes we'll tell you in the app.
